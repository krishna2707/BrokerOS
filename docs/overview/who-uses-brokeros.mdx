## Brokerage Sales Team (Internal)

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Pre-Sales Agent" icon="phone">
    Call leads using the Android auto-dialer, log call outcomes, follow up with interested buyers, and book site visits.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Pre-Sales Manager" icon="user-tie">
    Set daily call targets for each telecaller, keep an eye on connect rates, reassign stalled leads, and review team performance.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Sales Executive" icon="briefcase">
    Meet buyers in person, run GPS-verified site visits, negotiate prices, reserve units, and confirm bookings.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Sales Manager" icon="chart-line">
    Oversee the sales team, approve discounts that go above the standard threshold, and track the full sales pipeline.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Post-Sales Executive" icon="file-check">
    Take over once a booking is confirmed: collect documents, arrange the home loan, draft the agreement, and manage possession.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Post-Sales Manager" icon="clipboard-list">
    Oversee the post-sales pipeline, push on loan disbursement issues, and make sure every customer gets their keys on time.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

---

## Channel Partner (CP) Team

<CardGroup cols={3}>
  <Card title="Sourcing Manager" icon="user-plus">
    Recruit and onboard external brokers. Verify their RERA, GST, and PAN. Pitch projects and commission structures to bring them on board.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Closing Manager" icon="handshake">
    Based at the project site. Greets broker-referred clients, runs tours, negotiates the final price, and signs off on bookings.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Channel Partner Head" icon="sitemap">
    Oversees the entire external broker network, approves commission tier upgrades, and signs off on settlement payouts.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Support & Operations

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Marketing Manager" icon="bullhorn">
    Run voice campaigns, WhatsApp broadcasts, SMS and email campaigns, and manage all ad platform integrations.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Finance Officer" icon="calculator">
    Log payments, generate invoices, record expenses, process builder commission invoices, and disburse broker settlements.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Executive Access

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Business Manager" icon="binoculars">
    Full visibility across both Brokerage and CP lines. Tracks P&L, lead conversions, and team quotas from a high level.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Director" icon="crown">
    Board-level read and strategic approval access. Reviews corporate performance, margin analysis, and audit logs.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Admin" icon="shield">
    Manages system configuration, user accounts, role assignments, integration setup, and audits. Full access to everything.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>
