## The Short Version

**BrokerOS** is an enterprise operating system for real estate brokerage firms.

It handles everything a brokerage needs from the moment a lead comes in, all the way to handing over the keys to a buyer's new home. At the same time, it manages an external network of real estate brokers (called Channel Partners) who bring their own clients.

Think of it as the entire brain of a real estate company, running on one platform.

---

## Two Businesses. One Platform.

BrokerOS runs **two separate businesses** side by side under one roof:

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Direct Brokerage" icon="building" href="/brokerage/leads/lifecycle">
    Your **in-house sales team** calls leads, books site visits, and closes property deals directly with buyers.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Channel Partner (CP) Network" icon="handshake" href="/channel-partner/overview">
    Your **external broker network** brings their own clients to your projects. You track their deals, verify their credentials, and pay their commissions.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

These two businesses share the same database and inventory, but are **completely isolated** from each other

## What Does BrokerOS Actually Do?

Here is everything happening inside the platform:

<CardGroup cols={3}>
  <Card title="Lead Management" icon="users" href="/brokerage/leads/lifecycle">
    Capture leads from ads, portals, and CSV uploads. Assign them automatically to your team. Track every call, follow-up, and visit.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Auto-Dialer (Android)" icon="phone-volume" href="/mobile/auto-dialer/architecture">
    Your telecalling team dials leads directly from an Android phone. The app records calls, writes out transcripts using AI, and saves everything to the CRM.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Site Visits" icon="map-pin" href="/brokerage/sales/site-visits">
    Make sure sales agents actually visit properties with buyers. The mobile app tracks their live GPS location at the project site to confirm the visit.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Bookings & Payments" icon="file-signature" href="/brokerage/bookings/creation">
    Lock units, collect tokens, generate payment demand letters, manage home loans, draft agreements, and hand over keys.
  </Card>
  <Card title="AI Voice Campaigns" icon="microphone-lines" href="/marketing/voice/overview">
    Call hundreds of leads automatically using AI voice callers on real phone lines. The AI speaks with buyers naturally, answers questions, and finds interested leads.
  </Card>
  <Card title="WhatsApp Marketing" icon="whatsapp" href="/marketing/whatsapp/overview">
    Send official WhatsApp announcements, create auto-reply chatbots, and let your whole sales team answer buyer chats from one shared business number.
  </Card>
  <Card title="SMS & Email Campaigns" icon="envelope" href="/marketing/sms/overview">
    Send SMS text messages and designed HTML emails with photos. See who opened your message and who clicked on links in real time.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Ad Lead Ingestion" icon="rectangle-ad" href="/marketing/ads/overview">
    Connect Facebook, Instagram, and Google Ads directly to BrokerOS. Whenever someone fills out an ad form, their details show up in your CRM within seconds.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Channel Partner Network" icon="network-wired" href="/channel-partner/overview">
    Manage outside property brokers. Register their agency, verify their RERA documents, track which deals they bring in, and calculate their commission payouts.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>
