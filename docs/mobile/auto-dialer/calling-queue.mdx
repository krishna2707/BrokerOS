## What is the Calling Queue?

The calling queue is the prioritized list of prospective buyers a pre-sales agent is assigned to contact during their shift. Mounted directly on the home screen of the `PRE_SALES` workspace (`apps/mobile/app/(dashboard)/pre-sales/index.tsx`), it eliminates manual spreadsheet lookups and ensures high-priority prospects are contacted immediately.

The queue is dynamically generated by the backend based on:
1. **Uncontacted Leads**: Newly ingested leads assigned to the telecaller (`status = PENDING` or `NOT_CONNECTED`).
2. **Scheduled Follow-Ups**: Prospects previously scheduled for follow-up on today's date (`FollowUp.scheduledAt = TODAY`).
3. **Daily Performance Targets**: Daily cold-call targets assigned by the Pre-Sales Manager (`ManagerTask.targetCalls`).

---

## Queue Prioritization Hierarchy

The mobile application sorts available leads according to urgency:

| Priority Rank | Category | Selection Rationale |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **1 (Highest)** | **Follow-ups Due Today** | Explicit commitment made to the homebuyer to contact them today. |
| **2** | **HOT Leads** | High buying intent (budget confirmed, site visit requested). |
| **3** | **WARM Leads** | Engaged buyers comparing floor plans or financing options. |
| **4** | **COLD / New Leads** | Fresh marketing inquiries requiring initial qualification. |

## Dialing & Disposition Workflow

<Steps>
  <Step title="Review Lead Details">
    The queue card displays the prospect's name, phone number, project of interest, inquiry source (Meta Ads, Google, Website), and notes from previous calls.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Trigger Native Call">
    The telecaller taps **Call Next**. The custom native module invokes `dialNumber(phoneNumber)`, initiating an outbound telephone call directly through the device's SIM card.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Live Call Tracking">
    The mobile app detects the `onCallStarted` event. The live call timer begins on screen, and the agent's status shifts to **On Call** across manager dashboards.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Call Completion & Disconnection">
    When either party disconnects, the native module captures the `onCallEnded` event and displays the **Call Disposition Modal**.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Select Disposition Outcome">
    The telecaller selects the outcome of the interaction:

    | Disposition | Next System Action |
    | :--- | :--- |
    | **Interested** | Marks lead as HOT. Prompts for Site Visit scheduling or follow-up date. |
    | **Site Visit Scheduled** | Triggers creation of a `SiteVisit` record for the Sales Executive team. |
    | **Call Back Later** | Opens date/time picker. Schedules future reminder in `FollowUp` table. |
    | **Not Connected / Busy** | Increments attempt counter. Retains lead in queue for later attempt. |
    | **Not Interested / Lost** | Marks lead as lost, requiring mandatory reason selection. |
    | **Wrong Number** | Flags contact information for data hygiene verification. |

    Telecallers can also dictate or type interaction notes before saving.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Automatic Queue Progression">
    Submitting the disposition syncs the call record with the backend and automatically advances to the next lead in the queue.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Daily Target Tracking

At the top of the pre-sales workspace, agents see their live target progress widget:

```
Daily Target:   80 calls
Calls Made:     48 / 80  [==============......] 60%
Connected:      32 calls
Follow-Ups Set: 6 leads
```

Daily targets are assigned each morning by the Pre-Sales Manager via the `ManagerTask` module. If an agent falls behind expected hourly pacing, the progress indicator displays an amber alert; meeting or exceeding targets illuminates a green achievement indicator.

## Automated Follow-Up Reminders

When a telecaller sets a follow-up callback:
1. The record is persisted in the database linked to the `Lead` and `User`.
2. At 9:00 AM on the scheduled date, Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) transmits a push notification alert.
3. The lead is placed at the top of the telecaller's calling queue (Priority 1).
4. Any follow-ups not completed by the end of the day are flagged in the Pre-Sales Manager's daily performance exceptions report.
